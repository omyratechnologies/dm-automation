# Gemai logo system

## Core idea

The **Signal G** combines three product truths in one compact mark:

- the circular stroke is an ongoing customer conversation;
- the open `G` makes the Gemai name recognizable;
- the integrated arrow is the fast, automated outbound reply.

The shape deliberately avoids camera, Instagram, Meta infinity, robot, and generic chat-bubble imagery. This keeps the identity independent and suitable for Meta App Review.

## Primary lockup

Use the gradient mark with the `Gemai` wordmark. Use `gemai-logo-light.svg` on light backgrounds and `gemai-logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds. The application renders the same lockup through `GemaiLogo`.

## Mark and app icon

Use `gemai-mark.svg` for square surfaces such as Meta, Clerk, GitHub, social profiles, and app stores. Use `gemai-mark-monochrome.svg` only when gradients or color are unavailable.

## Color

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Signal indigo | `#5B6AF0` |
| Intelligence violet | `#8B5CF6` |
| Ink | `#111827` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

Do not recolor the primary mark with Instagram's gradient. Keep enough contrast for the white signal path to remain clear.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width on every side.
- Minimum digital size: `16px` for the mark and `88px` wide for the full lockup.
- At sizes below `24px`, use the square mark without the wordmark.

## Do not

- stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the mark;
- separate the arrow from the `G` loop;
- place the mark on a busy image without a solid container;
- combine it with Instagram or Meta logos as a single lockup.
